Getting started

Who is this for?

Creators and business owners who want content that actually pays. Coaches, service providers, performers, local businesses and personal brands all fit.

Do I need a big following already?

No. My skits account went from zero to 100K in four days. You need a system, not a head start.

Do I have to show my face?

It helps, but it's not a must for every niche. On the call we'll figure out the format that fits you and your business.

How it works

What happens after I apply?

You book a call. We look at where you are, what's missing and what it would take to get you paid from your content. If it's a fit, I'll show you how we'd work together. If not, you still leave with clarity.

Is this about paid ads?

No. Everything starts organic. Once a video proves itself for free, we can put money behind it. That's exactly how Dogs Do Magic sold out theatres.

How much time will this take each week?

We build the system around your schedule, not the other way around. Consistency matters more than hours.

Results and expectations

How fast will I see results?

Honest answer: it depends on your niche, your offer and how consistently you execute. Some people see traction in weeks. Real income takes sustained work.

Can you guarantee followers or sales?

Nobody honest can. What you get is the same system that built 11M followers across three different niches, and someone in your corner running it with you.
